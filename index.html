Research

Job Market Paper
The Smartphone Effect: Mobile Broadband and Health at Birth
With Hamid Noghanibehambari

This paper uses the rollout of iPhone and AT&T’s exclusive partnership in 2007 alongside county-level mobile broadband coverage data in a difference-in-differences framework to identify causal effects of smartphone access on maternal health behaviors and infant birth outcomes. Key finding: greater broadband coverage after 2007 reduced average birth weight by approximately 3.9 grams per standard deviation increase in coverage.

Public Prescription Drug Insurance and Welfare: Evidence from Medicare Part D

Examines welfare effects of public prescription drug insurance using the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (MEPS) for 2000–2012 and an instrumental variables estimation strategy.
